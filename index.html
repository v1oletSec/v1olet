Practice

A small team that stays in the tooling.

v1olet is an independent offensive security team. We assess web applications, APIs, and infrastructure, simulate adversaries against live detection, and take software apart to find the bug classes automated tooling never reaches.

The same operators who run client engagements compete year-round under the v1olet tag, against fields of five hundred to seven thousand teams. It keeps the practice sharp, and it means our record is a matter of public record rather than a case study we wrote about ourselves.
