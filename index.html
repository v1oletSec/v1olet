How it works

Four stages, no surprises on the invoice.

  1. 01

    Scope

    We agree the targets, the rules of engagement, and the window. You get a fixed price and a named lead before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Test

    Testing runs against a shared channel. Anything critical reaches you the hour we confirm it, not in a report six weeks later.

  3. 03

    Report

    Each finding lands with reproduction steps, a proof of concept, business impact, and remediation your engineers can act on.

  4. 04

    Retest

    Once you have shipped the fixes we verify them and reissue the report. Included in the original price.
